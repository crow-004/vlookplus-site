FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Matching runs on your computer. Only if you press the AI button, the names of the disputed rows (not the whole file) are sent to the AI service you configured with your own key.

What can I match?

Anything described by text: product names, company names, addresses, item codes with descriptions — in Latin or Cyrillic. Two .xlsx files: the one to fill and the reference list.

How accurate is it?

Every result carries a status — confident, review or none — so you only check what needs checking. Thresholds are adjustable. Accuracy depends on how different the two lists are; try the demo on your own data.

Does it need internet?

Only for the optional AI step. The key arrives by e-mail and is verified offline on your PC.

What happens after the demo ends?

Matching and export pause until you enter a license key. Your files and settings are untouched.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10/11 today. Other platforms on request.
